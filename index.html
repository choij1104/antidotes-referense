Evidence tier

A
FDA-approved specific antidote with strong or definitive evidence.
B
Approved or guideline-endorsed standard of care; moderate evidence.
C
Off-label, supported by case series and expert consensus.
D
Investigational, contested, or not obtainable in the US.
—
No specific antidote exists. Supportive care is the treatment.

Compiled and fact-checked against FDA labeling, CDC/CHEMM, AHA 2023 toxicology guidelines, ASRA 2020 LAST checklist, EXTRIP recommendations, and current primary literature. Where a product's regulatory or supply status was in motion at the time of review — bentracimab, IV silibinin, physostigmine — the entry says so rather than implying availability.

This is decision support for trained clinicians, not a protocol and not a substitute for a regional poison center. Confirm every dose against your own institution's formulary before administration. Pediatric dosing is weight-based and is only given where it differs materially from the adult approach.

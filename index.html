Every entry carries the pitfall, because in poisoning the antidote is rarely the hard part — the timing, the endpoint, and the contraindication are. Doses are adult unless noted and assume a normally functioning consultant on the other end of the phone.

Evidence tier

A
FDA-approved specific antidote with strong or definitive evidence.
B
Approved or guideline-endorsed standard of care; moderate evidence.
C
Off-label, supported by case series and expert consensus.
D
Investigational, contested, or not available in the US.
—
No specific antidote exists. Supportive care is the treatment.

Compiled and fact-checked against FDA labeling, CDC/CHEMM, AHA 2023 toxicology guidelines, ASRA 2020 LAST checklist, EXTRIP recommendations, and current primary literature through August 2026. Where a product's regulatory status was in motion at the time of writing — bentracimab, IV silibinin, cobinamide — the entry says so rather than implying availability.

This is a decision-support reference for trained clinicians, not a protocol and not a substitute for a regional poison center. Confirm every dose against your own institution's formulary before administration. Pediatric dosing is weight-based and is only given where it differs materially from the adult approach.
